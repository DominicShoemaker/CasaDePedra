A private house for a Copacabana stay

Casa de Pedra is a four-bedroom vacation rental in Copacabana, Rio de Janeiro. It is designed for travelers who want the space and privacy of an entire house instead of dividing a family or group across several hotel rooms. The property accommodates up to 10 guests and includes four bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms, a full kitchen, laundry facilities, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, a dedicated workspace, a private hot tub and a BBQ area.

The house is in a quieter residential pocket of Copacabana near Rua Pompeu Loureiro and Rua Barão de Ipanema. Cantagalo Metro station is approximately 500 meters away, Copacabana Beach approximately 800 meters away, and Forte de Copacabana approximately 2 kilometers away. Cafés, bakeries, pharmacies, markets and everyday services are available throughout the surrounding neighborhood.

Why choose a house for rent in Copacabana?

A private vacation house can be especially useful for travelers who want shared living areas, a real kitchen, laundry, work space and the ability to stay together under one roof. Casa de Pedra provides those features while keeping the group close to the beach, the metro and the restaurants and shops of Copacabana.

For longer stays, the kitchen and laundry reduce dependence on restaurants and hotel services. For shorter Rio trips, the layout lets guests gather in the living areas while still having separate bedrooms. The dedicated workspace and reliable connectivity also make the property suitable for guests combining vacation days with remote work.
