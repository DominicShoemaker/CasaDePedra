A villa-style alternative to a Rio hotel

Casa de Pedra is an entire private residence in Copacabana with the scale and amenities travelers often look for when searching for a luxury villa in Rio de Janeiro. The house has four bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms and room for up to 10 guests, giving families and groups a shared base without giving up private sleeping areas.

Rather than a collection of separate hotel rooms, guests have a living room, full kitchen, laundry, outdoor space, a private hot tub, BBQ area and a dedicated workspace. Air conditioning and televisions are available throughout the house, and the property is set on a quieter residential street while remaining close to the beach, metro, shops and restaurants.

Space for groups

Copacabana location

Approximately 800 m to Copacabana Beach and 500 m to Cantagalo Metro.

For families, friends and longer stays

A larger residence is useful when a group wants to share meals, plan outings together or spend time at home between activities. The kitchen supports breakfast and group meals, the laundry is convenient for beach trips and longer stays, and the office gives remote workers a dedicated place to work without taking over a bedroom or dining table.

The house is also positioned for guests who want to move around Rio without relying entirely on cars. Cantagalo Metro is approximately 500 meters away. Copacabana Beach is approximately 800 meters away, and Forte de Copacabana is approximately 2 kilometers away toward the Ipanema side of the neighborhood.
