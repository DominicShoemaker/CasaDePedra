A Private House for Your Copacabana Stay

More space than a hotel room, with the group together under one roof.

Casa de Pedra is a private four-bedroom vacation rental villa in Copacabana, Rio de Janeiro, accommodating up to 10 guests. The house combines generous shared spaces with four separate bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms, a full kitchen, laundry, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, a dedicated workspace, a private hot tub and BBQ area.

It is a practical base for families and groups who want an entire house rather than several hotel rooms. The property is in a quieter residential pocket of Copacabana near Rua Pompeu Loureiro and Rua Barão de Ipanema, with Cantagalo Metro approximately 500 meters away, Copacabana Beach approximately 800 meters away and Forte de Copacabana approximately 2 kilometers away.
